Diagnosis Variable Comparison: WORST_GOLDDX vs WORST_DISCRETEDX

This page compares the two diagnosis variables to help determine which is better for outcome classification:

  • diagnosis_3cat (WORST_GOLDDX): “GOLD” reviewer histology diagnosis - manually reviewed

Recommendations

Based on this comparison:

  1. Agreement Rate: Check the overall agreement rate between GOLDDX and DISCRETEDX
  2. Missing Data: Compare which variable has fewer missing values
  3. CIN2+ Classification: The key determination for Disease classification
  4. Clinical Relevance: GOLDDX is manually reviewed and may be more accurate, but DISCRETEDX may be more complete

Note: The current analysis uses GOLDDX (diagnosis_3cat) for outcome classification. If DISCRETEDX shows better data quality or completeness, consider switching to DISCRETEDX-based classification.
