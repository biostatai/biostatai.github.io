Relative Abundance by Key Sample Characteristics

This section visualizes the relative abundance of microbial taxa (at the Genus level) across different sample groups defined by key metadata variables, using the microeco package. The plots show abundances averaged across samples within each group. Plots involving combined factors (interactions) may become visually complex due to the large number of groups.
